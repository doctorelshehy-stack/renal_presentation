Section 03
The Glomerular Filtration Barrier
Capillary endothelium, basement membrane and foot processes of podocytes form the filtration barrier
  • The endothelial cells of glomerular capillaries are covered by a basement membrane, which is surrounded by podocytes.
  • The capillary endothelium, basement membrane and foot processes of podocytes form the so-called filtration barrier.
  • The endothelium is fenestrated and is freely permeable to water and to small solutes such as urea and glucose — and even to small protein molecules.
  • Slit diaphragms bridge the filtration slits between the foot processes.
Filtration barrier: fenestrated endothelium → basement membrane → podocytes
