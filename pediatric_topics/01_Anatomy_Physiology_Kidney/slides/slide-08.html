Section 03
Ultra-structure of the Nephron — Renal Corpuscle
Figure (2): Ultra structure of the renal corpuscle
  • The term ultra filtration refers to the passive movement of an essentially protein-free fluid from the glomerular capillaries into Bowman's space.
  • The glomerulus is a network of capillaries supplied by the afferent arteriole and drained by the efferent arteriole.
  • Capillaries are covered by epithelial cells called podocytes, forming the visceral layer of Bowman's capsule.
  • The visceral cells are reflected at the vascular pole to form the parietal layer of Bowman's capsule.
  • The space between both layers is Bowman's space, which at the urinary pole becomes the lumen of the proximal tubule.
