Section 02
Gross Anatomy — The Kidney
Figure (1): structure of the human kidney
  • On the cut surface of a bisected kidney two regions are evident: an outer cortex and an inner medulla.
  • The cortex and medulla are composed of nephrons — the functional units of the kidney.
  • The pelvis represents the upper expanded region of the ureter, which carries urine from the pelvis to the urinary bladder.
  • The pelvis divides into two or three open-ended pouches, the major calyces, that divide into minor calyces.
