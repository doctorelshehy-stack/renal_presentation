Wrap-Up
Key Takeaways
  • The kidneys are regulatory and excretory organs: fluid/osmolarity, electrolytes, acid-base, waste excretion and hormone production.
  • Gross anatomy: outer cortex, inner medulla, pelvis dividing into major and minor calyces, draining via the ureter.
  • The nephron is the functional unit; the renal corpuscle (glomerulus + Bowman's capsule) starts urine formation by ultrafiltration.
  • The filtration barrier = fenestrated endothelium + basement membrane + podocyte foot processes.
  • Ultrafiltrate is protein-free except molecules ≤ 68,000 MW; the placenta is the major excretory organ in fetal life.
