Section 03
Heme-Positive — Hemoglobinuria vs Myoglobinuria
Two conditions that give a positive dipstick heme test
1. Hemoglobinuria
  • With acute hemolytic anemia.
  • CBC shows fragmented RBCs & reticulocytosis.
  • Hemoglobin in urine.
  • Plasma color red.
2. Myoglobinuria
  • Due to rhabdomyolysis (skeletal muscle injury) in viral myositis, crush, prolonged fits.
  • High serum creatine kinase.
  • Plasma color clear.
Key discriminating point
Both give red/brown urine with a positive dipstick heme — the plasma color (red in hemoglobinuria vs clear in myoglobinuria) and CBC findings (fragmented RBCs & reticulocytosis) help differentiate the two.
