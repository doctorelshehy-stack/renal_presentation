Section 01
Detection — Dipstick & 24-Hour Collection
Urine dipstick test
  • Offers a qualitative assessment of urinary protein excretion.
  • Detects primarily albuminuria; less sensitive for other forms of proteinuria.
24 hours urine collection
  • Upper limit of normal in healthy children: 150 mg/24 hr (4 mg/m²/hr).
  • Abnormal: > 4 mg/m²/hr.
  • Nephritic range: 4–40 mg/m²/hr.
  • Nephrotic range: more than 40 mg/m²/hr.
Dipstick reporting scale
Negative  •  Trace (10–20 mg/dL)  •  1+ (30 mg/dL)  •  2+ (100 mg/dL)  •  3+ (300 mg/dL)  •  4+ (1000–2000 mg/dL)
