Section 02
Glomerular Proteinuria
Causes
Acute post-infectious glomerulonephritis, Focal segmental glomerulosclerosis, Mesangial proliferative glomerulonephritis, Membranous nephropathy, Membranoproliferative glomerulonephritis, Lupus nephritis, IgA nephropathy, Henoch-Schönlein purpura nephritis, Sickle cell nephropathy
AspectDetail
MechanismDue to increased permeability of the glomerular capillary wall.
LevelRange from less than 1 g to more than 3 g / 24 hr.
Type — SelectiveLoss of plasma proteins of molecular weight up to and including albumin.
Type — NonselectiveLoss of albumin and of larger molecular weight proteins such as IgG.
AssociationsHypertension, hematuria, or renal dysfunction.
