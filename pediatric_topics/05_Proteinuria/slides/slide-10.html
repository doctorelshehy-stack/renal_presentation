Section 02
Initial Evaluation of Persistent Fixed Proteinuria
  1. Measurement of serum creatinine.
  2. 24-hr urinary protein excretion.
  3. Serum albumin level.
  4. C3 level.
Low-grade proteinuria (150–1,000 mg/24 hr) with normal findings
Renal biopsy may not be indicated — the underlying process may be transient and resolving (e.g., acute post-infectious glomerulonephritis), or specific pathologic features of a chronic renal disease may not yet be apparent.
Such patients should have periodic re-evaluation consisting of
Physical examination, Blood pressure determination, Urine analysis, Measurement of serum creatinine, and 24-hr urinary protein excretion.
