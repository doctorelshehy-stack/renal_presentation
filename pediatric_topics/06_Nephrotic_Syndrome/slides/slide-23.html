Section 08
Steroid Therapy — Induction & Tapering
Induction of remission
  • Children with presumed minimal change disease should receive prednisone.
  • Dose: 60 mg/m²/day (maximum daily dose, 60 mg).
  • Divided into two to three doses.
  • For at least 4 consecutive weeks.
Response & alternate-day dosing
  • 80% to 90% of children respond to steroid therapy.
  • Response = urine trace or negative for protein for 3 consecutive days → steroid responsive N.S.
  • After the initial 4–6 wk course, taper prednisone: 40 mg/m²/day, every other day as a single morning dose.
  • Then slowly tapered and discontinued over the next 2–3 mo.
