Section 06
Diagnosis — Urine Analysis
  1. Color → yellowish, frothy.
  2. Volume → normal (may be Oliguria; may be polyuria with steroid ttt).
  3. Specific gravity → high.
  4. Proteinuria → Heavy (Nephrotic level): dipstick 3+ or 4+; urinary protein excretion exceeds 40 mg/m²/hr; spot urine protein to creatinine ratio exceeds 2.0.
  5. Microscopic examination → microscopic hematuria may be present in 20% of children; casts → waxy (lipoid) & hyaline.
