Section 04
Clinical Manifestations
  • Idiopathic nephrotic syndrome is more common in males than females (2:1) and most commonly appears between the ages of 2 and 6 yr.
  • The initial episode and subsequent relapses may follow minor infections and, occasionally, reactions to insect bites, bee stings.
  • Children usually present with mild edema, initially noted around the eyes and in the lower extremities. With time, the edema becomes generalized, with development of ascites, pleural effusions, and genital edema.
  • Anorexia, irritability, abdominal pain, and diarrhea are common.
  • Hypertension and gross hematuria are uncommon.
