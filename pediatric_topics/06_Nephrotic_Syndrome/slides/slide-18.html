Section 06
Diagnosis — Biochemical & Renal Biopsy
Biochemical
  • Serum creatinine is usually normal, but may increase because of diminished renal perfusion resulting from contraction of the intravascular volume.
  • Serum albumin generally < 2.5 g/dL.
  • Low total serum protein < 4.5 g/dL.
  • Serum cholesterol and triglyceride levels are elevated.
  • C3 and C4 levels are normal.
