Section 07
Supportive Treatment — Diuretics & Salt-Free Albumin
3) Oral Diuretics
  • No data support their safety or efficacy, yet used by many clinicians because of the possibility of increasing the risk of thromboembolic complications.
  • Use should be judicious and supervised by pediatric nephrologists.
  • A swollen scrotum may be elevated with pillows to enhance removal of fluid by gravity.
Salt free Albumin
IV 25% human albumin (0.5 g/kg/12 hr).

Indications:
• Massive generalized oedema (anasarca) with respiratory difficulty → albumin + furosemide (1–2 mg/kg IV).
• Hypovolemic shock → albumin alone.

Mandates close monitoring of volume status, serum electrolytes and renal function.
Heavy use of diuretics alone in NS carries risk of
