Section 01
Definition of Nephrotic Syndrome
Epidemiology
Primarily a pediatric disorder — 15 times more common in children than adults. Incidence: 2–3 / 100,000 children per year. The vast majority of affected children have steroid-sensitive minimal change disease.
Characteristic features (the tetrad)
1
Heavy Proteinuria (> 3.5 g/24 hr in adults or 40 mg/m²/hr in children)
2
Hypoalbuminemia (< 2.5 g/dL)
3
Generalized edema
4
Hyperlipidemia — hypercholesterolemia (> 250 mg/dL)
