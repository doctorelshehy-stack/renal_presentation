Section 04
Clinical Approach to Diagnosis
  1. Is this red urine “hematuria”?
  2. What are the causes of hematuria?
  3. Clinical approach of hematuria: search for signs suggestive of each cause.
  4. Laboratory approach of hematuria: localize hematuria (glomerular or extraglomerular).
For all cases
  1. Analysis of fresh urine sample for casts, bacteria, crystals, RBCs shape.
  2. Abdominal ultrasound for stones, tumors, malformations.
  3. Renal functions tests.
  4. Blood picture.
  5. Coagulation profile.
