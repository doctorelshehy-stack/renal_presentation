Section 04
Laboratory Approach by Site of Hematuria
For Glomerular hematuria — reduced C3 in
  1. Post infectious glomerulonephritis.
  2. Systemic lupus nephritis.
  3. Nephritis with chronic infection.
  4. Membrano proliferative glomerulonephritis.
For post-streptococcal GN
  1. ASO titre.
  2. Anti DNase titre.
  3. Throat or skin culture.
For lupus nephritis
  1. ANA.
  2. Anti Double strand DNA.
For extra glomerular hematuria
  1. Urine culture for urinary tract infections.
  2. 24 hours urine Calcium, uric acid & oxalate for Crystalluria, stones.
  3. Cystogram & renal scan for hydronephrosis.
  4. Renal Doppler for renal vein thrombosis.
