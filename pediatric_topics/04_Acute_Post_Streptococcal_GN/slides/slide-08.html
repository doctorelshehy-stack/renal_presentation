Section 03
Pathogenesis — Immune Complex Mediated
Mechanism
Although morphologic studies and a depression in the serum complement (C3) level strongly suggest that PSGN is mediated by immune complexes, the precise mechanisms by which nephritogenic streptococci induce complex formation remain to be determined.
Streptococcal infection
→
Abs → Ag–Ab + complement (C3) immune complexes
→
Deposited in GBM (subepithelial humps)
→
Edema, Hypertension, Oliguria
