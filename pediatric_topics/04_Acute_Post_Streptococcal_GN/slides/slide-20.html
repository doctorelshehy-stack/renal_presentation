Section 07
Treatment of APSGN
A) General measures
  1. Bed rest: only indicated during the oliguric phase of illness.
  2. Fluid restriction: Fluid intake = UOP + 400 ml / m² of body surface area.
  3. Protein and salt restriction.
  4. High carbohydrate diet to provide adequate calories.
B) Specific measures
  1. Management of the acute effects of renal insufficiency and hypertension.
  2. Eradication of streptococcal infection by a 10-day course of systemic antibiotic therapy with penicillin — antibiotic therapy does not affect the natural history of glomerulonephritis.
