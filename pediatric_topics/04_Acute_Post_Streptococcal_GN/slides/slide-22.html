Wrap-Up
Key Takeaways
  • APSGN = acute, specific, self-limited GN after group A β-hemolytic streptococcal infection: gross hematuria, edema, hypertension, oliguria.
  • Throat strains 4 & 12 (winter/spring, 1–2 wk, ASO elevated) vs skin strain 49 (summer, 2–3 wk, ASO may be normal).
  • Immune complexes deposit as subepithelial humps on the GBM → edema, hypertension, oliguria.
  • Diagnosis: smoky/cola urine with dysmorphic RBCs and casts, nephritic proteinuria, low C3, rising ASO/DNase B titers.
  • Emergencies: hyperkalemia, hypertensive encephalopathy, CHF, RPGN. Biopsy only for RPGN, nephrotic proteinuria, or atypical features.
  • Treatment: fluid = UOP + 400 ml/m², salt/protein restriction, 10-day penicillin; prognosis excellent (>95% recover, recurrence rare).
