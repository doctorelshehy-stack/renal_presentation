Wrap-Up
Key Takeaways
  • AKI = rapid decline (hours–days) in GFR with impaired waste excretion and loss of water/electrolyte/acid-base regulation; 2–3% of pediatric admissions, 8% of NICU infants.
  • pRIFLE staging: Risk (GFR ↓25%) → Injury (↓50%) → Failure (↓75% or <35 ml/min) → Loss (>4 wk) → ESRD (>3 mo).
  • Three categories: prerenal (low perfusion, no damage), intrinsic (parenchymal damage: GN, ATN, interstitial nephritis), postrenal (bilateral obstruction).
  • Key labs: prerenal — Uosm >500, UNa <20, FENa <1%; intrinsic — Uosm <350, UNa >40, FENa >2%.
  • Treatment: hospitalization, treat the cause, correct electrolytes/acid-base; recovery often heralded by a polyuric phase.
