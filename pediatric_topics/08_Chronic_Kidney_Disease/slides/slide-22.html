Wrap-Up
Key Takeaways
  • CKD = kidney structure/function abnormality or GFR < 60 ml/min/1.73 m² for > 3 months (KDOQI).
  • Staging: 1 (>90) → 2 (60–89) → 3 (30–59) → 4 (15–29) → 5 (<15 = ESRD).
  • Etiology by age: <5 yr — congenital (hypoplasia/dysplasia, obstructive uropathy); >5 yr — glomerular diseases and inherited disorders.
  • Progression driven by hemodynamic perturbations, hypertension, proteinuria, podocyte injury, cytokines, profibrotic factors.
  • Findings: uremia, acidosis, Na/K disorders, renal osteodystrophy, growth retardation, anemia, bleeding, infection, neurological/GI symptoms, hypertension, hyperlipidemia, cardiomyopathy, glucose intolerance.
  • Management: fluids/electrolytes, Bicitra/bicarbonate (HCO₃ > 22), protein 2.5 g/kg/24 hr, rHuGH (−2 SD), phosphate binders + vitamin D, rHuEPO, ACE inhibitors, dose adjustment (Schwartz formula), dialysis at stage 5.
