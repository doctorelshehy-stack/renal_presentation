Diagnostic Modalities & Staging Tools

Imaging, biopsy and staging evaluation

Core Diagnostic Steps

  • Transrectal ultrasonography (TRUS)
  • Prostate biopsy guided by TRUS
  • Diagnostic transurethral resection of the prostate (TURP)

Local Staging (T-Staging)

  • Based on magnetic resonance (MR) imaging
  • Number and sites of positive prostate biopsies, tumour grade and serum PSA level provide further information
  • Dynamic contrast-enhanced MR (DCE-MRI) helpful in equivocal cases

Metastasis Staging (M-Staging)

  • Small metastases (< 5 mm): pelvic lymph node dissection (PLND) remains the only reliable staging method in clinically localized PCa
  • Skeletal metastasis best assessed by bone scan
  • In equivocal cases: PET/CT or whole-body MRI are an option
PSA is the key biochemical marker; TRUS-guided biopsy confirms histology; MR imaging stages the primary tumour; bone scan and PLND address skeletal and nodal spread respectively.
