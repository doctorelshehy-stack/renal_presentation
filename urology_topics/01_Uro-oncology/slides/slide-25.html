Upper Urinary Tract · 4/5

Staging — T Classification

Primary tumour staging for renal pelvis and ureter

T Stage Definitions

StageDefinition
TXPrimary tumour cannot be assessed
T0No evidence of primary tumour
TaNon-invasive papillary carcinoma
TisCarcinoma in situ
T1Tumour invades subepithelial connective tissue
T2Tumour invades muscularis
T3Renal pelvis: beyond muscularis into peripelvic fat or renal parenchyma — Ureter: beyond muscularis into periureteric fat
T4Tumour invades adjacent organs or through the kidney into perinephric fat

Key Points

  • Ta/Tis/T1: non-invasive or superficially invasive — amenable to endoscopic or topical therapy
  • T2 and beyond: invasion of the muscularis — radical surgery is the standard
  • T3 differs by site: peripelvic fat / renal parenchyma (pelvis) vs periureteric fat (ureter)
  • T4: adjacent organs or perinephric fat involvement
The T stage, together with grade, multifocality and renal function, drives the choice between radical nephroureterectomy and endoscopic management.
