Renal Tumors · 3/4

RCC Staging — The 2017 TNM Classification

Primary tumour classification

StageDefinition
TX / T0Primary tumour cannot be assessed / no evidence of primary tumour
T1Tumour ≤ 7 cm in greatest dimension, limited to the kidney — T1a: ≤ 4 cm; T1b: > 4 cm but ≤ 7 cm
T2Tumour > 7 cm in greatest dimension, limited to the kidney — T2a: > 7 cm but < 10 cm; T2b: > 10 cm, limited to the kidney
T3Tumour extends into major veins or perinephric tissues but not into the ipsilateral adrenal gland and not beyond Gerota fascia — T3a: renal vein or segmental branches, pelvicalyceal system, perirenal and/or renal sinus fat; T3b: grossly into vena cava below diaphragm; T3c: grossly into vena cava above the diaphragm or invades the wall of the vena cava
T4Tumour invades beyond Gerota fascia (including contiguous extension into the ipsilateral adrenal gland)
T-staging separates tumours limited to the kidney (T1–T2), those extending into veins or perinephric tissue within Gerota fascia (T3), and those beyond Gerota fascia (T4).
