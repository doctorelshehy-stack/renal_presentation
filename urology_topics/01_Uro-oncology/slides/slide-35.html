Testicular Tumors · 3/3

Diagnosis, Staging & Treatment

TNM classification adapted from UICC 2016, 8th edition

Diagnosis

  • Ultrasonography — scrotal duplex ultrasound
  • Computed tomography with contrast of the chest, abdomen and pelvis (to detect lymph nodes and other distant metastases)
  • Positron emission tomography (PET) scan

Treatment

  • Start with ipsilateral radical orchiectomy
  • Radiotherapy
  • Chemotherapy
  • Retroperitoneal lymph node dissection

pT — Primary Tumour Staging

StageDefinition
pTx / pT0Cannot be assessed / no evidence of primary tumour (e.g., histological scar in testis)
