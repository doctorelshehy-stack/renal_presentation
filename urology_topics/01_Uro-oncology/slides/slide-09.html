Prostate Cancer · 6/8

Methods of Androgen Deprivation

Sites of action of hormonal therapy: LHRH agonist/antagonist · orchiectomy · anti-androgens

Bilateral Orchiectomy

  • Surgical castration — removes testicular androgen production

LHRH Agonists

  • Long-acting agonists: busereline, gosereline, leuproreline, triptoreline
  • Medical castration via pituitary LHRH-receptor stimulation

LHRH Antagonists

  • In contrast to agonists: bind immediately and competitively to LHRH receptors in the pituitary gland

Anti-Androgens

  • Compete with testosterone and DHT at the receptor level in the prostate cell nucleus
  • Thus promoting apoptosis and inhibiting PCa growth

Estrogens — Mechanisms of Action

  • Down-regulation of LHRH secretion
  • Androgen inactivation
  • Direct suppression of Leydig cell function
  • Direct cytotoxicity to the prostate epithelium (in-vitro evidence only)

Complete Androgen Blockade (CAB)

  • Combination of castration (surgical or medical) with anti-androgens
  • Commonly known as complete / maximal / total androgen blockade
