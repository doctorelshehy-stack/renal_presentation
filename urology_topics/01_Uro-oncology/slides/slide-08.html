Hormonal Therapy — The Androgen Axis

Androgen deprivation therapy (ADT) and the hypothalamic-pituitary-gonadal axis

A

Hypothalamus

Secretes luteinizing hormone-releasing hormone (LHRH)

B

Anterior Pituitary

LHRH stimulates release of luteinizing hormone (LH) and follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH)

C

Leydig Cells of the Testes

LH stimulates Leydig cells to secrete testosterone

D

Prostate Cell

Testosterone → 5-α-dihydrotestosterone (DHT) by 5-α-reductase; DHT is ~10× more powerful as an androgenic stimulant

Why Deprivation Works

  • If prostate cells are deprived of androgenic stimulation, they undergo apoptosis (programmed cell death)
  • Any treatment that ultimately suppresses androgen activity is androgen deprivation therapy (ADT)

Two Routes of Androgen Deprivation

  • Suppress secretion of testicular androgens — surgical or medical castration
  • Inhibit action of circulating androgens — at the receptor in prostate cells, using competing compounds known as anti-androgens

Complete Androgen Blockade (CAB)

The two methods (surgical or medical castration + anti-androgens) can be combined to achieve complete (or maximal / total) androgen blockade (CAB).
