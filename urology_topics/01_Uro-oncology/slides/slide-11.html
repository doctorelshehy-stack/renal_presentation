Decision-making integrates Gleason score, life expectancy and tumour risk category. T1: watchful waiting, active surveillance, radical prostatectomy or radiotherapy per risk. T2: radical prostatectomy is standard for life expectancy > 10 years; hormonal therapy is reserved for palliation.
