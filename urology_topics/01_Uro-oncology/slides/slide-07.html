Gleason Score — Histological Grading

The current standard for grading adenocarcinoma of the prostate

Principles

  • Over 95% of prostate cancers are adenocarcinomas
  • Gleason score is the current standard for grading adenocarcinoma of the prostate
  • Relies on the low-power appearance of the glandular architecture under the microscope
  • Grades adenocarcinoma into five grades (patterns 1–5)
  • Score = sum of the two most common patterns of tumour growth found
  • Score ranges 2–10: 2 = least aggressive, 10 = most aggressive

Patterns 1–5 — Glandular Architecture

Grade 1

well-formed, closely packed glands

Grade 2

separated, still well-formed glands

Grade 3

infiltrative glands, variable size

Grade 4

fused, poorly formed glands

Grade 5

no glandular differentiation, sheets of cells
