Injuries to the Posterior Urethra — Differential Diagnosis & Complications

Differential Diagnosis

  • Bladder rupture may be associated with posterior urethral injuries in approximately 20% of cases
  • Cystography cannot be done preoperatively, since a urethral catheter should not be passed
  • Careful evaluation of the bladder at operation is necessary

The Three Complications

  • Stricture
  • Impotence
  • Incontinence

Complication Rates — Primary Repair vs Delayed Reconstruction

ComplicationPrimary repair & anastomosisSuprapubic cystostomy + delayed repair
Strictureabout 50%reduced to about 5%
Impotence30–80% (mean ~50%)reduced to 30–35%
Incontinenceone-third of patientsreduced to less than 5%
Delayed reconstruction after suprapubic drainage dramatically reduces all three complications.
