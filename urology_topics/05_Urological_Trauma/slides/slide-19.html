Injuries to the Urinary Bladder — Treatment

Surgical Measures

  • Shock and haemorrhage should be treated
  • A lower midline abdominal incision is used
  • Pelvic hematoma, which is usually lateral, should be avoided — entering it can result in increased bleeding from release of tamponade and in infection of the hematoma, with subsequent pelvic abscess
  • The bladder should be opened in the midline and carefully inspected
  • After repair, a suprapubic cystostomy tube is usually left in place to ensure complete urinary drainage and control of bleeding

Extraperitoneal Rupture

  • Can be successfully managed with urethral catheter drainage only — typically, 10 days will provide adequate healing time
  • Large blood clots in the bladder or injuries involving the bladder neck should be managed surgically
  • The bladder is opened in the midline, carefully inspected, and lacerations closed from within
