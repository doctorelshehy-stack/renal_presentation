Urinary Incontinence · 3/12

Types — Continuous, Nocturnal, Post-Micturition & Pharmacologic

Continuous Urinary Incontinence

  • Continuous leakage of urine
  • Most common causes — 1 — urinary fistulas; 2 — ectopic ureter

Nocturnal Enuresis & Post-Micturition Dribbling

  • Nocturnal enuresis — loss of urine occurring during sleep
  • Post-micturition dribbling — involuntary loss of urine immediately after passing urine

Pharmacologic Urinary Incontinence

  • Loss of urine due to effect of medications taken by the patient
  • Diuretics — do not cause incontinence but may exacerbate incontinence symptoms
  • Anticholinergics — may impair bladder emptying
  • Tricyclic antidepressants (e.g., imipramine) — may increase bladder outlet obstruction and contribute to urinary retention and overflow incontinence
  • Doxazosin or tamsulosin — may cause or worsen stress incontinence
A medication review is a mandatory step — drugs may cause or unmask incontinence.
