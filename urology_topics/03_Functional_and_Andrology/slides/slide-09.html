Urinary Incontinence · 6/12

Investigations — Laboratory & Imaging

Laboratory

  • Urinalysis will detect hematuria, pyuria, bacteria or glucosuria

Imaging — US with Post-Void Residual (PVR)

  • Important for patients in whom decreased bladder emptying is known or suspected

Imaging — Lateral Cystogram

  • Normally, the vesicourethral junction is opposite the lower third of the pubic bone
  • It moves 0.5–1.5 cm with straining
Elevated PVR raises the suspicion of retention/overflow; cystogram demonstrates bladder neck descent.
