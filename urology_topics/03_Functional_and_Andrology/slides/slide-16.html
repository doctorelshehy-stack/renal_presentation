Benign Prostatic Hyperplasia · 1/10

Anatomy of the Prostate

Gross Anatomy

  • Approximately 70% glandular elements and 30% fibromuscular stroma
  • Normal prostate weighs 18 g; measures 3 cm in length, 4 cm in width, and 2 cm in depth
  • Traversed by the prostatic urethra
  • Ovoid shape with anterior, posterior and 2 inferolateral surfaces; narrowed apex inferiorly and broad base superiorly

Zonal Anatomy

  • A — Anterior (ventral) fibromuscular stroma — fibrous, non-glandular
  • B — Glandular portion — 1 — peripheral zone: about 70% of the glandular part; 2 — central zone: about 25% of the glandular prostate; 3 — transition zone and periurethral glands
BPH arises predominantly in the transition zone and periurethral glands — the zone that surrounds the prostatic urethra.
