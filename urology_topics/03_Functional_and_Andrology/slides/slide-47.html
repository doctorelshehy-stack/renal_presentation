Chancroid

Etiology

  • Caused by the gram-negative bacterium Haemophilus ducreyi
  • Infection leads to anogenital ulceration and lymphadenitis with progression to bubo formation

Diagnosis

  • A definitive diagnosis of chancroid requires culture on media not routinely available
  • The CDC suggests a probable diagnosis can be made if the patient has one or more painful ulcers with no evidence of T. pallidum or HSV infection
