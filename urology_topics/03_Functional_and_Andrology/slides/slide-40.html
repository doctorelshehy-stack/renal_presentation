Premature Ejaculation — Definition, Classification & Causes

International Society of Sexual Medicine 2014

Definition (ISSM 2014)

  • 1 — Ejaculation that always or nearly always occurs prior to or within 1 minute of vaginal penetration (lifelong PE), or a clinically significant and bothersome reduction in latency time, often to about 3 minutes or less (acquired PE)
  • 2 — Inability to delay ejaculation on all or nearly all vaginal penetrations
  • 3 — Negative personal consequences: distress, bother, frustration, and/or avoidance of sexual intimacy

Prevalence & Classification

  • Prevalence — varies from 5–30% of the male population
  • Lifelong (primary) PE — manifests from the first sexual encounter
  • Acquired (secondary) PE — develops at some point in life after a previous normal ejaculation experience
  • Variable PE — irregular (or situational) early ejaculation — considered normal
  • Subjective PE — perceived early ejaculation with normal ejaculation time

Causes

  • Lifelong PE — hyposensitivity of the 5-HT2c and/or hypersensitivity of the 5-HT1a receptor
  • Acquired PE — sexual performance anxiety; psychological and relationship problems; erectile dysfunction; prostatitis; hyperthyroidism; during recreational drugs withdrawal
Serotonergic receptor sensitivity (5-HT2c / 5-HT1a) underlies lifelong PE — the rationale for SSRI therapy.
