Syphilis — Diagnosis & Treatment

Diagnosis

  • Darkfield examination of lesion exudate can detect Treponema pallidum
  • Non-treponemal serology — venereal disease research laboratory test (VDRL); rapid plasma reagin test (RPR)
  • Treponemal serology — fluorescent treponemal antibody absorption test (FTA-ABS); T. pallidum particle agglutination test (TP-PA)
  • Diagnosis of syphilis requires two laboratory serologic tests: a non-treponemal test and a treponemal test

Treatment

  • Intravenous penicillin G is the preferred drug for treating all stages of syphilis

Key Teaching Points

  • Non-treponemal tests (VDRL, RPR) are screening tests; treponemal tests (FTA-ABS, TP-PA) confirm
  • Both test types together establish the diagnosis
  • Penicillin G covers all stages — primary, secondary, latent and tertiary
Darkfield microscopy is the direct diagnostic method; serology is the mainstay of screening and confirmation.
