Benign Prostatic Hyperplasia · 8/10

Treatment — Watchful Waiting & Medical Therapy

Watchful Waiting

  • Instructions to the patient to avoid: cold weather; excessive fluid intake; sexual excitement; some drugs — diuretics, antispasmodics and alpha-adrenergic stimulants (as common cold drugs)

Medical Therapy

  • Alpha-adrenergic blockers — relax bladder neck muscles and muscle fibres in the prostate, making urination easier; alfuzosin, doxazosin; more specific alpha-1 blockers: tamsulosin, silodosin; usually work quickly in men with relatively small prostates
  • 5-alpha-reductase enzyme inhibitors — prevent conversion of testosterone to the more active dihydrotestosterone; shrink prostate size by about 25%; finasteride and dutasteride (more potent); may take three to six months to be effective
  • Phosphodiesterase (PDE) 5 inhibitors — tadalafil (Cialis) — used for erectile dysfunction, can also treat prostate enlargement through relaxation of smooth muscles
  • Combination therapy — combined alpha-blocker + 5-ARI
  • Phytotherapy — acts as anti-inflammatory and decongestant
  • Treatment of any UTI — always address infection
Alpha-blockers act quickly; 5-ARIs shrink the gland over months — combination therapy serves larger prostates.
