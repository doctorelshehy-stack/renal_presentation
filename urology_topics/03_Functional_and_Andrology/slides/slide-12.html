Urinary Incontinence · 9/12

Cystourethroscopy & Treatment Overview

Cystourethroscopy

  • Visualizes the urethra and bladder — facilitates diagnosis of benign and malignant lesions, e.g., urethral diverticula
  • Observation of the female sphincteric unit to assess its ability to coapt and close
  • Evaluation of vesicovaginal fistula
  • Searching for extra-urethral causes of incontinence

Treatment Options — Overview

  • Lifestyle changes / behavioural modification
  • Pelvic floor muscle therapy
  • Oral pharmacologic agents: α-adrenergic agonists · serotonin-norepinephrine reuptake inhibitors · imipramine · estrogen
  • Continence pessary and vaginal inserts
  • Urethral bulking agents
  • Surgery: sling operation · colposuspension · artificial urinary sphincter · urine diversion
Treatment escalates from conservative (lifestyle, PFMT) through pharmacologic and devices to surgery.
