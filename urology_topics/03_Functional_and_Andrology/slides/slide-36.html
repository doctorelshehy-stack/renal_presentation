Erectile Dysfunction — Etiology

Psychogenic

  • Life stressors, and/or conflict with partner
  • Performance anxiety

Vasculogenic

  • Atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease, hypertension, obesity, lack of exercise, diabetes mellitus, radiotherapy to the pelvis

Neurogenic & Hormonal

  • Neurogenic — diabetes mellitus, stroke, spinal cord trauma, pelvic and urethral surgeries
  • Hormonal — diabetes mellitus, hypogonadism, panhypopituitarism

Drug-Induced & Anatomical

  • Drug induced — antihypertensives (thiazide, beta-blockers), antidepressants, antiandrogens, recreational drugs (heroin, cocaine, excessive alcohol)
  • Anatomical — Peyronie's disease, penile fracture, vascular trauma
