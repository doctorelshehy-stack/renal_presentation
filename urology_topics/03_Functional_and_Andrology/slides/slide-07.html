Urinary Incontinence · 4/12

Types — Transient Incontinence

Reversible causes that should be identified and corrected first

Delirium & Psychological Causes

  • Delirium
  • Psychological factors — e.g., severe depression or neurosis

Excessive Urine Output

  • High fluid intake
  • Diuretics
  • Metabolic abnormalities — hyperglycaemia, hypercalcemia
  • Fluid overload — congestive heart failure, peripheral venous insufficiency, hypoalbuminemia
  • Drug-induced peripheral oedema — calcium channel blockers, non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs)
  • Amantadine (drug for Parkinson disease and influenza)
  • Beta blockers

Stool Impaction

  • Stool impaction can precipitate transient incontinence — treat the impaction before further evaluation

Clinical Approach

Transient causes are reversible: delirium, psychological illness, excessive urine output and stool impaction. Correcting them may cure the incontinence without any further therapy.
