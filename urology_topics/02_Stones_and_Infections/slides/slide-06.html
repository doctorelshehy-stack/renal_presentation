Stone Disease · 3/16

Types & Incidence of Stones

Classification by anatomical site, opacity on plain X-ray, or composition

Composition-Based Classification

  • Calcium stones — calcium oxalates and calcium phosphates — 70–80%
  • Infected stones — struvite and appetite stones — 10–20%
  • Uric acid stones — 5–10%
  • Cystine stones — 1–2%
  • Miscellaneous — xanthine, silicate, matrix, indinavir stones and others — less than 5%

Basis of Classification

  • Anatomical site — e.g., renal, ureteric, bladder, urethral
  • Opacity on plain X-ray — radiolucent (e.g., uric acid, invisible on plain X-ray, requiring ultrasonography or non-enhanced CT) or radiopaque
  • Composition — the five main groups
