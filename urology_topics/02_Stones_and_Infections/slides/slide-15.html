Open Surgery & Management of Ureteric Calculi

Open Surgery

  • Indicated only if ESWL and PCNL have failed, are contraindicated, or are not available
  • Nowadays, only about 5% of patients will be candidates for open surgery

Pain Distribution of Ureteric Stones

  • Upper (proximal) third — flank pain with referred pain to the testicle
  • Lower (distal) third — flank pain, right/left lower quadrant pain, with radiation of pain to the scrotum or vulva

Management by Site

SiteOptions (in order)
Upper third1 — ESWL; 2 — Ureteroscopy with laser lithotripsy; 3 — open surgery
Middle third1 — Ureteroscopy with laser lithotripsy; 2 — ESWL; 3 — open surgery
Lower third1 — Ureteroscopy; 2 — ESWL; 3 — open surgery

Complications of Ureteroscopy

  • Iatrogenic trauma
  • Haematuria
  • Infection
  • Ureteric stricture
