Prostatitis, Chronic Pelvic Pain Syndrome & Epididymo-Orchitis

NIDDK/NIH Classification of Prostatitis

  • I — Acute bacterial prostatitis
  • II — Chronic bacterial prostatitis
  • III — Chronic abacterial prostatitis — chronic pelvic pain syndrome (CPPS): A. inflammatory CPPS (white cells in semen/EPS/VB3); B. non-inflammatory CPPS (no white cells)
  • IV — Asymptomatic inflammatory prostatitis (histological prostatitis)

Treatment of Prostatitis

  • Acute bacterial prostatitis — admission and parenteral antibiotics, then oral for 2–4 weeks
  • Chronic prostatitis — ciprofloxacin and levofloxacin are drugs of choice because of their favourable pharmacokinetic properties (4–6 weeks)

Epididymitis & Orchitis

  • Complications of epididymo-orchitis: abscess formation, testicular infarction, testicular atrophy, chronic epididymal induration and infertility
  • Antimicrobials should be selected on an empirical basis
  • Chlamydia — doxycycline, 200 mg/day
  • Gram-negative organisms — fluoroquinolones
EPS = expressed prostatic secretion; VB3 = post-massage urine sample. CPPS is the commonest prostatitis category in practice.
