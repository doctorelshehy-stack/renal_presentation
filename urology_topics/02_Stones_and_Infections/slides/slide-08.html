Complications of Urinary Stones

The Four Major Complications

  • Obstruction — leading to back pressure changes that can endanger renal function
  • Infection — with its sequelae
  • Deterioration — of renal function
  • Chronic irritation — that can lead to metaplasia and/or malignant transformation

Clinical Consequence Chain

Stone → obstruction → hydronephrosis → back-pressure nephropathy. Stone + infection → pyonephrosis (an emergency). Chronic irritation of the urothelium by a long-standing stone may trigger metaplasia and, rarely, malignant transformation.
