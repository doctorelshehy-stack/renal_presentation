Catheter Fever & Sepsis Syndrome in Urology (Urosepsis)

Catheter Fever — General Aspects

  • 1 — Written catheter care protocols are necessary
  • 2 — Health care workers should observe protocols on hand hygiene and use disposable gloves between catheterized patients
  • 3 — An indwelling catheter should be introduced under antiseptic conditions
  • 4 — Urethral trauma minimized by adequate lubricant and small catheter calibre
  • 5 — The duration of catheterization should be minimal

Urosepsis

  • SIRS (fever or hypothermia, hyperleucocytosis or leucopenia, tachycardia, tachypnoea) is the first event in a cascade to multi-organ failure
  • Sepsis or septic shock may follow
  • The prognosis of urosepsis is globally better than sepsis from other infectious sites
  • Mortality rate as high as 20–40%

Treatment of Urosepsis

  • Adequate life-supporting care
  • Appropriate and prompt antibiotic therapy
  • Adjunctive measures — sympathomimetic amines, hydrocortisone, blood glucose control, recombinant activated protein C
  • Optimal management of urinary tract disorders
