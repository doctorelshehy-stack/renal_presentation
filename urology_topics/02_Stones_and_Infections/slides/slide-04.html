Stone Disease · 1/16

Definition & Incidence

Definition

  • A urinary stone is a crystalline aggregate of different amounts of urinary crystals and an organic matrix
  • Urinary stones are polycrystalline aggregates of crystalloids and an organic matrix

Incidence

  • Urolithiasis affects 10–20% of the population
  • Males are 1–2 times more affected than females
  • Calculus disease is the 3rd most common urinary tract disease
  • High recurrence rate of 50–60% within 10 years of the first attack
The single most striking feature of stone disease is its recurrence — over half of patients form a new stone within a decade.
