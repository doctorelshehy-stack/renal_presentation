Prostate-Specific Antigen (PSA)

Nature & Values

  • A 34 kD glycoprotein enzyme produced by prostatic epithelial cells
  • Present in semen to liquefy the ejaculate
  • Some is absorbed into blood → 75% bound to plasma proteins while 25% is free
  • Values — semen level 0.5 to 5 mg/dL; blood level < 4 ng/mL

Factors That Increase PSA in Blood

  • Prostatitis
  • Prostate cancer
  • Enlarged prostate
  • Urethral catheterization and instrumentations
  • Prostate biopsy

Derivatives

  • Free/Total PSA — if the ratio < 0.25 this suggests malignancy
  • PSA velocity — absolute annual increase in serum PSA (ng/mL/year); cutoff > 0.75 ng/mL/yr
  • PSA density — serum total PSA divided by prostate volume (ng/mL/gram); cutoff ≥ 0.15 ng/mL/gram
  • Age-specific PSA — 40–49 yrs → 0–2.5; 50–59 → 3.5; 60–70 → 4.5; 70–79 → 6.5

Uses

  • Screening and early detection of prostate cancer
  • Follow-up of response to treatment after prostate cancer therapy
