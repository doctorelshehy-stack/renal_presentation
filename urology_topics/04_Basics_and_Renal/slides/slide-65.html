Transplantation · 6/5

Renal Transplantation — Surgical Complications 4–6

Complications 4–6

  • Lymphocoele — 5–15% within the first 3 months (lymph leaking from severed lymphatics); large collections — pain, ARF, frequency, ipsilateral lower extremity edema; can cause iliac vein thrombosis or PE; diagnosis: U/S; treatment: percutaneous drainage
  • Obstructive uropathy — 3–6% early post-transplant; extrinsic ureteral compression by lymphocoele or technical issues at the ureteric anastomosis; diagnosis: U/S (hydronephrosis); management: surgical
  • Renal artery stenosis — presents late; uncontrolled hypertension, allograft dysfunction, peripheral edema; diagnosis: U/S or MRA

Complication Pattern — Summary

  • First week — acute vascular occlusion (0.5–8%) — oligoanuria, ARF; renal vein thrombosis — graft tenderness, dark hematuria
  • Early post-op / anticoagulation — peritransplant haematoma (2–3%) — pain, falling Hb, rising creatinine, hyperkalemia
  • First month — urinary leak (2–5%) — extravasation, ARF, fever
  • First 3 months — lymphocoele (5–15%) — pain, ARF, frequency, ipsilateral edema
  • Early transplant — obstructive uropathy (3–6%) — hydronephrosis on U/S
  • Late — renal artery stenosis — hypertension, allograft dysfunction, edema
Vascular → haematoma → leak → lymphocoele → obstruction → stenosis: the six surgical complications in order of incidence.
