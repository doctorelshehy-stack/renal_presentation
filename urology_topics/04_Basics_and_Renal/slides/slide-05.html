The Kidneys — Overview

Position & Size

  • Lie obliquely along the borders of the psoas muscles
  • The right kidney is lower than the left (displaced by the liver)
  • Adult kidney weighs about 150 g; measures ~12 × 6 × 3 cm (left often slightly longer)
  • Extend from T12 to L3; the hilum lies at L1
  • Bean-shaped, indented medially (the renal sinus)
  • Each kidney contains about one million nephrons (glomerulus + tubules)

Support

  • Perirenal fat
  • Renal vascular pedicle
  • Abdominal muscle tone
  • Bulk of the abdominal viscera
  • The kidney and its strong capsule are surrounded by pararenal fat

Internal Structure

  • Outer cortex and inner medulla
  • Medulla: multiple medullary pyramids, each opening through its tip into a minor calyx
  • Minor calyces → three major calyces (upper, middle, lower) → renal pelvis → ureter
  • Renal lobe — a medullary pyramid plus its overlying cortex
  • Columns of Bertin — extensions of cortical tissue between the pyramids
  • Renal papilla — the tip of a pyramid that projects into a minor calyx
