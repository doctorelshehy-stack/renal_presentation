Renal Hilum, Pelvis & Renal Fascia

Renal Hilum (at the concave part of each kidney)

  • 1 — Renal vein exits (anteriorly)
  • 2 — Renal artery enters (posterior to the renal vein)
  • 3 — Renal pelvis exits (posterior to the artery)

Renal Pelvis

  • Funnel-shaped; lined with transitional epithelium with a smooth muscle and connective tissue wall
  • Continuous inferiorly with the ureter
  • Divides into major and minor calyces
  • Urine flow: collecting tubule → minor calyx → major calyx → renal pelvis → ureters → bladder

Renal (Gerota's) Fascia

  • Encloses the kidney with its surrounding fibrous and fatty capsules
  • Helps maintain organ position
  • Superiorly: continuous with the fascia of the inferior diaphragm
  • Medially: the left and right fascia blend with each other anterior to the abdominal aorta and IVC
  • Posterior layer blends with the fascia overlying the psoas
The hilum arrangement — vein anterior, artery middle, pelvis posterior — is a classic examination question.
