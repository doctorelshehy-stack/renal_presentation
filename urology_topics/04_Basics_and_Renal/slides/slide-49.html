AKI · 12/14

Management of AKI — Treat the Cause & Complications

Treat the Cause

  • Infections — administer antibiotics at renal doses
  • Intrinsic renal disease — review and adjust medications
  • Obstruction — consider catheters, calculus removal, nephrostomies, or surgery
Obstruction is the urologist's domain — relieve it before renal recovery is lost.

Treat Complications

  • Correct fluid imbalances: strict I/O's, daily weights, determine fluid balance goals daily, fluid selection or diuresis, readjust for urine output recovery, post-diuresis or dialysis
  • Electrolyte imbalances (low potassium/phosphorus diet)
  • Metabolic acidosis
  • Nutrition: adjust protein intake
  • Medication dosing: adjust for eGFR to avoid under- or overdosing; timing for dialytic therapy; reassess dosing for renal recovery or dialysis modality
  • Procedural considerations: prefer non-contrast CT, appropriate delay of contrast exposure, prophylaxis
