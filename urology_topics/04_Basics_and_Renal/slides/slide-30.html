Serum Creatinine & Creatinine Clearance

Uses

  • Serum creatinine — indicator for renal function; a rise indicates damage to a significant number of nephrons
  • Creatinine clearance — used to estimate glomerular filtration rate (GFR)
  • Formula — GFR = Urine creatinine (U) × Urine volume (V) / Plasma creatinine (P)
  • Normal GFR — 90–120 mL/min

Normal Values

  • Adult — 0.4 – 1.4 mg/dL
  • Children — 0.2 – 0.7 mg/dL
Creatinine clearance is the bedside estimate of GFR — the product of urinary concentration and flow divided by plasma level.
