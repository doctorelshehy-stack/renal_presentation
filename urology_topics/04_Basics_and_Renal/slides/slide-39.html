AKI · 3/14

Aetiology of AKI — Pre-Renal & Intrinsic

Pre-Renal (55%)

  • Adaptive response to volume depletion and hypotension; nephrons remain structurally intact
  • Hypovolemia
  • Decreased cardiac output: CHF, liver failure
  • Drugs affecting renal auto-regulation: NSAIDs, ACE-I/ARBs, cyclosporin

Renal / Intrinsic (40%)

  • Caused by cytotoxic, ischemic, or inflammatory insults leading to structural and functional damage
  • Glomerular — acute glomerulonephritis
  • Tubular / interstitium — ischemia, sepsis, nephrotoxic drugs
  • Vascular — vasculitis, TTP-HUS, malignant hypertension
  • Nephrotoxins — exogenous drugs (e.g., aminoglycosides) or endogenous substances (e.g., myoglobin)

Risk Factors for AKI

  • Advanced age (> 75 years)
  • Pre-existing conditions: CKD, cardiac failure, peripheral vascular disease, hypertension, hepatic disease, diabetes mellitus
  • Exposure to nephrotoxic medications
