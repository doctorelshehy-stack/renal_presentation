AKI · 10/13

When to Refer to a Nephrologist (Within 24 Hours)

Referral Criteria

  • A specialist diagnosis is suspected — vasculitis, glomerulonephritis, tubulointerstitial nephritis, or myeloma
  • AKI has no clear cause
  • Inadequate response to treatment or associated complications
  • AKI stage 3 is present
  • The patient has a renal transplant
  • Chronic kidney disease (CKD) stage 4 or 5 is present

Key Point

Early nephrology input changes outcomes in AKI — referral is mandated within 24 hours of detection when any of the six criteria are met. Urologists contribute by excluding and relieving obstruction, the only truly reversible cause.
Stage 3 AKI, transplant patients, unexplained AKI, suspected specialist diagnoses and inadequate response all mandate referral.
