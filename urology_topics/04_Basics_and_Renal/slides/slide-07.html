Relations, Blood Supply & Innervation

Relations of the Kidneys

  • Posterior surface — superiorly: diaphragm, pleura and lower two ribs; inferiorly (medial to lateral): psoas major, quadratus lumborum, transversus abdominis
  • Right kidney anterior — right suprarenal gland, liver, second part of the duodenum, right colic flexure, coils of small intestine
  • Left kidney anterior — left suprarenal gland, stomach, spleen, pancreas, left colic flexure and descending colon, jejunum

Vessels & Lymphatics

  • Arterial — renal arteries branch from the aorta at L1/L2 behind the pancreas and renal veins; enter at the hilum: anteriorly — apical, upper, middle, lower segments; posteriorly — posterior segment; no communication between segments
  • Venous — renal veins communicate widely, eventually forming 5 vessels that unite at the hilum; drain into the IVC
  • Lymphatic — para-aortic nodes at L1/L2; the surface of the upper kidney drains through the diaphragm into posterior mediastinal nodes

Innervation

  • Sympathetic: preganglionic cells in spinal cord T12/L1 → fibres to thoracic and lumbar splanchnic nerves
  • Postganglionic cells in coeliac, renal and superior hypogastric plexuses
  • Vasomotor function
