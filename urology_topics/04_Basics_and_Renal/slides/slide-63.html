Renal Transplantation — Function, Prognosis & Morbidity

Function & Prognosis

  • Living donor transplants usually function immediately after surgery
  • Delayed graft function: ~30% of cadaveric transplants — due to prolonged ischemic cold preservation; continued dialysis until the graft begins to function
  • Graft survival — living donor 95% at 1 year, 76% at 5 years; cadaveric 89% at 1 year, 61% at 5 years
  • Patient survival — living donor 98% at 1 year, 91% at 5 years; cadaveric 95% at 1 year, 81% at 5 years

Morbidity & Mortality

  • Infection: most common cause of morbidity and mortality in the first year
  • Hypertension: 75–85% of recipients; hyperlipidaemia: 60%
  • Cardiovascular disease: 15.8–23%; diabetes mellitus: 16.9–19.9% (new-onset DM associated with corticosteroid use)
