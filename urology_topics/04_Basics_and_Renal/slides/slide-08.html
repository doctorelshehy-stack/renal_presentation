Anatomy · 5/12

The Adrenal Glands

Overview

  • Each kidney is capped by an adrenal gland
  • Enclosed within Gerota's (perirenal) fascia
  • Each adrenal weighs about 5 g
  • Right adrenal is triangular; left adrenal is more rounded and crescentic
  • Each gland: cortex — chiefly influenced by the pituitary gland; medulla — derived from chromaffin tissue

Relations

  • Right adrenal lies between the liver and the vena cava
  • Left adrenal lies close to the aorta; covered on its lower surface by the pancreas; superiorly and laterally related to the spleen

Arterial Supply

  • Three arteries: one from the inferior phrenic artery, one from the aorta, and one from the renal artery

Venous Drainage & Lymphatics

  • Right adrenal vein drains into the inferior vena cava
  • Left adrenal vein drains into the left renal vein
  • Lymphatic vessels accompany the suprarenal vein and drain into lumbar lymph nodes
The left adrenal vein → left renal vein is the key landmark for adrenal surgery.
