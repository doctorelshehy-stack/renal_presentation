Basics & Renal — Key Takeaways

Anatomy: kidneys T12–L3 (hilum L1, right lower), 150 g, ~1 million nephrons; hilum — vein anterior, artery, pelvis posterior; Gerota fascia; renal artery divides into 5 non-communicating segments; bladder — oblique intramural ureter = anti-reflux mechanism; prostate 20 g (Lowsley 5 lobes / McNeal zones); urethra 4 cm female / 15 cm male; spermatic cord contents and pampiniform plexus (left → left renal vein).
Embryology: pronephros → mesonephros (Wolffian duct) → metanephros (ureteric bud + metanephrogenic blastema); urorectal septum divides the cloaca; trigone from incorporated mesonephric ducts.
Congenital anomalies: renal agenesis (bilateral lethal, unilateral compatible), adult PKD (AD, 30–50 yrs) vs infantile (AR, lethal at birth), hypoplasia vs dysplasia, malrotation, ectopia (pelvic/iliac/abdominal/thoracic/crossed), horseshoe kidney; ureterocele (cobra head), UPJO (stenosis/aberrant vessels/aganglionic), bladder exstrophy, posterior urethral valves, hypospadias (never circumcise; repair 6–18 months), cryptorchidism (treat before 1 year; laparoscopy for non-palpable; Fowler–Stephens).
Investigations: first morning urine avoids orthostatic proteinuria; microscopy — RBC < 3/hpf, WBC < 5/hpf, casts (RBC = GN, WBC = infection, cellular = ATN); GFR = U×V/P (normal 90–120); PSA < 4 ng/mL, free/total < 0.25 suggests malignancy, velocity > 0.75, density ≥ 0.15; KUB, IVU phases (scout → nephrogram → urogram → prone → full bladder → post-micturition), VCUG (reflux, PUV keyhole sign), ascending urethrogram.
AKI: creatinine ↑ ≥ 0.3 mg/dL in 48 h or 1.5× baseline in 7 days, urine < 0.5 mL/kg/hr × 6 h; KDIGO I–III; pre-renal 55% (reversible with fluids), intrinsic 40% (ATN, AGN, AIN, vascular), post-renal 5% (relieve obstruction fast); dialysis for K⁺ > 7, pH < 7.2, refractory pulmonary oedema, pericarditis, encephalopathy, BUN > 150.
CKD: damage or GFR < 60 for > 3 months; KDIGO G1–G5; diabetes 38% of ESRD; ACEi/ARB first-line for proteinuria (NICE: < 130/80 with proteinuria); HbA1c < 7%; protein 0.6–0.8 g/kg/day; ESA when Hb < 10 (target 10.5–12).
Transplantation: preferred for ESRD; ABO/HLA compatibility; UW solution; wet ischemia < 30 min; LDN standard (left side); graft survival living 95%/76% vs cadaveric 89%/61% at 1/5 years; triple immunosuppression; infection = leading cause of 1st-year mortality; macrolides reduce CNI doses; malignancy risk 2–5-fold; cost-effective long term.

Anatomy explains embryology, anomalies explain disease, and investigations bridge the two — the foundation of all urology.
