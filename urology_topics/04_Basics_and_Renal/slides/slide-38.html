Stages of Acute Kidney Injury (KDIGO)

StageSerum creatinineUrine output
Stage I1.5–1.9 times baseline, or > 0.3 mg/dL increase< 0.5 mL/kg/hr for 6 hours
Stage II2–2.9 times baseline< 0.5 mL/kg/hr for 12 hours (two 6-hour blocks)
Stage III3 times baseline, or > 4 mg/dL, or initiation of renal replacement therapy (dialysis)< 0.3 mL/kg/hr for 24 hours, or anuria for 12 hours
Staging by the worst criterion (creatinine OR urine output); Stage III includes the need for dialysis.
