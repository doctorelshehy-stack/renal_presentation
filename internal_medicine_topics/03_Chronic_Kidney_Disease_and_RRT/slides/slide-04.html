Definitions

CKD is defined as abnormalities of kidney structure or function, present for > 3 months with implications for health, and is classified based on cause, GFR category and albuminuria category.
Chronic Renal Failure (CRF): GFR is < 60 ml/min/1.73 m² for ≥ 3 months (Grade 3 CKD).
End Stage Renal Disease (ESRD): a permanent and irreversible renal impairment which necessitates renal replacement therapy = Grade 5 CKD.
