Summary

CKD Essentials

  • Structure/function abnormalities > 3 months.
  • Classified by cause + GFR (G1–G5) + albuminuria (A1–A3).
  • ESRD = Grade 5 = needs RRT.

Clinical Picture

  • GIT: anorexia, vomiting, uremic fetor.
  • CVS: CHF, HTN, pericarditis, atherosclerosis.
  • Hematological: anemia, bleeding, infections.
  • Bone: CKD-MBD & renal osteodystrophy.
  • Neuro, skin, urinary & electrolyte disturbances.

Investigations

  • Urine analysis, KFT (urea, creatinine, eGFR), electrolytes, A#F1E8D9.
  • Iron profile, iPTH, skeletal survey.
  • Serology, renal imaging, biopsy (not if small atrophic kidneys).

Conservative Treatment

  • Correct reversible causes & underlying disease.
  • Control BP, glucose, proteinuria (ACEI/ARBs), lipids, lifestyle.
  • Diet, GIT, CVS, MBD, anemia & pruritus management.
  • Educational programs.

Dialysis

  • PD: peritoneum as membrane; safe, preserves residual function.
  • HD: efficient, short time; vascular-access surgical complications + acute/chronic medical complications.
  • Indications: AKI, uremic complications, toxins, ESRD (GFR ≤ 10).

Transplantation

  • Living or deceased donor.
  • Contraindications: refusal, psychosis, sepsis, malignancy, etc.
  • Complications: rejection, immunosuppression, infection, malignancy.
