Clinical Features — Cardiovascular

Cardiovascular

  • 1. CHF and pulmonary edema; dyspnea, orthopnea, increased jugular venous pressure, lower limb edema due to volume overload.
  • 2. Arrhythmias; due to electrolyte abnormalities.
  • 3. Hypertension.
  • 4. Pericarditis and pericardial effusion.
  • 5. Accelerated atherosclerosis.

NB: Absence of hypertension in a CKD patient may be due to: salt wasting nephropathy e.g. PCKD · use of anti-hypertensive drugs · volume depletion e.g. GIT loss or diuretic overdose.
