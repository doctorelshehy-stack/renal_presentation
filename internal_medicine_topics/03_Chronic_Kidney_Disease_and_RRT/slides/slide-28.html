Treatment — Mineral Bone Disease (2)

Calcimimetic agents (cinacalcet) — inhibits iPTH secretion.
Vitamin D analogs (alfacalcidol/calcitriol; 0.25–2 µg daily) according to serum Ca, PO₄ and iPTH levels. Hold if there is hypercalcemia and/or hyperphosphatemia. Treatment with vitamin D is recommended for renal patients with:
  • 1. Total serum calcium level < 9.5 mg/dl.
  • 2. Serum phosphate level < 4.6 mg/dl.
  • 3. Elevated serum PTH level.
Parathyroidectomy in severe hyperparathyroidism (PTH > 800 pg/dL) not responding to medications.
