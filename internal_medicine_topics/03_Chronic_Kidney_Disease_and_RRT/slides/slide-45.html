Kidney Transplantation — Definition, Donors & Contraindications

Definition

  • Surgical implantation of a kidney obtained from either a healthy kidney donor or a brain stem dead cadaver (deceased).

Types of Donors

  • Living (blood related, unrelated) donor.
  • Deceased (cadaveric) donor.

Contraindications

  • 1. Patient’s refusal.
  • 2. Psychosis.
  • 3. Elderly (relative contraindication).
  • 4. Active sepsis.
  • 5. Unstable cardiovascular disease.
  • 6. Severe respiratory distress.
  • 7. Cerebro-vascular hemorrhage.
  • 8. Advanced liver disease; unless combined liver and kidney transplantation.
  • 9. Malignancies.
  • 10. Un-repairable urological abnormalities.
