Indications of Dialysis

I) Temporary (acute / urgent) dialysis

  • 1. In AKI: many nephrologists advise early dialysis in AKI in all patients, at least for some periods of time, to simplify management which enhances recovery and minimizes development of complications.
  • 2. Control of some uremic symptoms and complications (in both AKI and CKD) as:
  • • Refractory hyperkalemia.
  • • Intractable metabolic acidosis.
  • • Progressive fluid overload unresponsive to diuretics.
  • • Pericarditis and pleuritis.
  • • CNS complications.
  • • GIT complications.
  • • Bleeding due to uremia.
  • 3. Removal of dialyzable toxins & drugs as; alcohols, aspirin, lithium, contrast dyes.

II) Permanent (chronic, regular or maintenance) dialysis

  • Grade 5 CKD (ESRD) as replacement therapy when GFR ≤ 10 ml/min, which may correspond to a serum creatinine of 8–10 mg/dL.
