Causes — 2. Intra-renal (Intrinsic) Causes

1. ATN — Acute Tubular Necrosis

  • a. Hemodynamic: cardiovascular surgery, sepsis, prolonged pre-renal cause.
  • b. Toxic: antimicrobials, iodide contrast agents, immunosuppression or antineoplastic agents, organic solvents, heavy metals, and radiation.
  • c. Intratubular deposits: Acute UA nephropathy, myeloma, severe hypercalcemia, primary oxalosis, sulfadiazine.
  • d. Organic pigments (endogenous nephrotoxins): myoglobin (rhabdomyolysis), hemoglobinuria (intravascular hemolysis).

2. Acute GN

e.g. RPGN.

3. Acute interstitial nephritis

Drug / infection mediated tubular-interstitial inflammation.

4. Renal vascular occlusion

Arterial / venous thrombosis, vasculitis, cortical necrosis.
