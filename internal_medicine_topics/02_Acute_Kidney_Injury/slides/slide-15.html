Investigations — 1. Urine Examination

Urine volume / color

  • Urine volume / 24 h.
  • Urine color: Reddish brown or cola-colored urine → myoglobin or Hb, especially in the setting of a positive dipstick and no red blood cells on microscopic examination.

Urinary red blood cells

  • Eumorphic (normal shape) → bleeding along the collecting system.
  • Dysmorphic or red cell casts → GN.

Urinary casts

  • Muddy brown casts → ATN — the presence of tubular cell casts and oxalate crystals supports the diagnosis of ATN.
  • White blood cell casts or white blood cells → pyelonephritis or acute interstitial nephritis (AIN) — the presence of urine eosinophils is helpful in establishing the diagnosis of AIN.
