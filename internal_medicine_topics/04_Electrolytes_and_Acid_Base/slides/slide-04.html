Overview of Acid-Base Disturbances — Primary Events

Overview of Acid-Base Disturbances — the primary event in each of the four disturbances

Disturbance Primary event
Metabolic acidosis Primary reduction of plasma HCO3−, leading to decrease in pH stimulating ventilation to decrease CO2 (low pH, CO2 and HCO3−)
Metabolic alkalosis Primary increase of plasma HCO3−, leading to increase in pH inhibiting ventilation to increase CO2 (high pH, CO2 and HCO3−)
Respiratory acidosis Primary retention of CO2, leading to decrease in pH enhancing renal retention of HCO3− (low pH, high CO2 and HCO3−)
Respiratory alkalosis Primary loss of CO2, leading to increase in pH enhancing renal excretion of HCO3− (high pH, low CO2 and HCO3−)

NB: The primary event (H+ gain/loss or CO2 retention/loss) triggers a physiologic compensatory response by the other organ system (lungs or kidneys) to defend the pH.
