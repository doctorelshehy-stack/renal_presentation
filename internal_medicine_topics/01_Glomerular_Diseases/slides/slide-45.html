Ch 07 · Diabetic Nephropathy

Clinical Features — Stages

StageDescription
Stage I — Hyperfiltration / Hypertrophy stageIncrease in GFR by 20–40% above age-matched control subjects. Hypertrophy of the kidneys by ultrasound. Clinically, there may be polyuria.
Stage II — Silent stageNormal GFR (in most patients). Normal urinary albumin excretion (< 30 mg/day). Early structural renal damage.
N.B.: About 30–50% of patients will proceed to stage III.
Stage III — Incipient nephropathyGFR starts to decline. Microalbuminuria (30–300 mg/day) — 5–10 years after the onset of DM. Early hypertension or rise of BP above the previous reading.
Stage IV — Overt nephropathyProgressive reduction of GFR. Overt proteinuria > 0.5 gm/day. Hypertension.
Stage V — ESRDESRD requiring renal replacement therapy. Presence of other complications of diabetes: retinopathy, neuropathy, CHF, cerebrovascular disease & peripheral vascular disease.

Type 2 DM — differences

In type 2 DM:
• Hyperfiltration stage is rarely detected
• Microalbuminuria is frequently present at the diagnosis of DM
• Hypertension is usually present at the time of diagnosis of nephropathy
