Ch 03 · Nephrotic Syndrome

Definition & Causes

Definition

N.S. is a clinical syndrome characterized by:

1. Heavy proteinuria ≥ 3.5 gm/d/1.73 m² body surface area, or urinary protein/creatinine ratio > 3.5 in adults.

2. Hypoalbuminemia ≤ 2.5 gm/dl

3. Edema

4. Hyperlipidemia (hypercholesterolemia)

Causes

All causes of acute GN.

Primary: MCD, MGN, MPGN, MCGN, or FSGN.

Secondary to: infection, multisystem disease, malignancy, drugs, metabolic or familial causes.

Contrast: Nephrotic syndrome = heavy proteinuria (≥ 3.5 gm/day) with hypoalbuminemia, edema and hyperlipidemia — in contrast to nephritic syndrome where proteinuria is < 3.5 gm/day and hematuria dominates.
