Ch 02 · Acute GN (Nephritic Syndrome)

Clinical Features — Edema, Hypertension, Oliguria & Associated Disease

3. Edema

• Early → eyelids (puffiness) — as this area is of low tissue pressure
• Then progresses to involve the legs and other parts

Due to salt and water retention because of:
– Increase reabsorption of filtered Na+ at the distal tubules
– Decrease GFR → decrease salt delivery to tubules

4. Hypertension

Due to salt & water retention, i.e. volume-dependent hypertension.

5. Oliguria

Urine output < 300 ml/day.

Due to decreased GFR.

B. Manifestations of the Associated Specific Disease

Manifestations of the associated specific disease, e.g.:
• Infection (PSGN)
• SLE
• Vasculitis
• Malignancy
