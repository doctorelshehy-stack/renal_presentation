Ch 03 · Nephrotic Syndrome

Renal Biopsy — Indications & Contraindications

Renal biopsy

Indications: histological diagnosis and to plan therapy.

In children

  • Steroid-resistant, steroid-dependent and frequent relapses
  • Children with renal impairment

In adults

Most adults.

Biopsy NOT indicated in:

• Young children with selective proteinuria, normotensive & benign urinary sediment → MCD
• Long-standing DM (> 10 years in type 1, > 5 years in type 2) with retinopathy or neuropathy
• Patients under drug therapy such as penicillamine
