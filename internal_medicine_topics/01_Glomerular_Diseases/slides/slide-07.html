Microscopic: Renal biopsy

Renal biopsy is the definitive tool — each modality answers a different question.
MethodValue
LM (light microscopy)The histopathological type / The severity & degree of disease
EM (electron microscopy)Define site of immune deposition: subendothelial, subepithelial, mesangial
IF (immunofluorescence)The type of immune deposition e.g. IgA, C3, C4.
IC GN → deposits appear diffuse granular pattern.
Anti-GBM Ab → deposits appear smooth linear pattern.
