Ch 05 · Post-Streptococcal GN

Investigations

Evidence of streptococcal infection

• Antibodies to streptococcal exo-enzymes: ASO, DNAase, BNDase and hyaluronidase
• Decrease of CH50 & C3 during the acute phase, then returns to normal within 8 weeks of nephritis (transient hypocomplementemia)

Evidence of acute GN

ModalityFinding
Urine examinationDysmorphic red cells, red cell casts & proteinuria (< 3 gm/day)
BloodTransient increase of serum cholesterol (in NS), decreased serum albumin, renal impairment
Renal biopsy — LMDiffuse endocapillary proliferative GN ± few crescents
Renal biopsy — EMSubepithelial electron-dense deposits (humps) and variable degrees of mesangial deposits
Renal biopsy — IFGranular deposits of C3 & IgG

Biopsy triad: Subepithelial humps on EM + granular C3 & IgG on IF + transient low C3 (recovers < 8 weeks) → PSGN.
