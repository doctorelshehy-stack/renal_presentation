Ch 02 · Acute GN (Nephritic Syndrome)

Definition

Definition

Acute GN is a clinical syndrome characterized by acute onset of:
1. Hematuria
Macroscopic or microscopic
2. Proteinuria
< 3.5 gm/d/1.73 m² body surface area
3. Hypertension
4. Edema
5. Oliguria
< 300 ml/day in severe cases
Nephritic vs Nephrotic
Proteinuria is non-nephrotic range (< 3.5 gm/day) in acute GN.

Contrast: The nephritic syndrome (acute GN) is dominated by hematuria, whereas the nephrotic syndrome is dominated by heavy proteinuria ≥ 3.5 gm/day.
