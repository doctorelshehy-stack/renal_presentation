Ch 06 · Lupus Nephritis

Pathological Classes — ISN/RPS Classification

ClassNameDescription
IMinimal mesangial LNNormal glomeruli by LM, and mesangial immune deposits by IF or EM.
IIMesangial proliferative LNMesangial hypercellularity and/or matrix expansion of any degree, in addition to mesangial immune deposits.
IIIFocal proliferative LN< 50% of glomeruli are involved.
IVDiffuse proliferative LN> 50% of glomeruli are involved.
VMembranous LNDiffuse (global) glomerular capillary wall thickening plus continuous subepithelial immune deposits.
VIAdvanced-stage LNGlobal glomerulosclerosis of ≥ 90% of the total glomeruli, without evidence of active lesion.

Remember: 6 major classes based upon LM, IF, and EM studies of renal biopsy, according to the International Society of Nephrology / Renal Pathology Society (ISN/RPS) classification.
