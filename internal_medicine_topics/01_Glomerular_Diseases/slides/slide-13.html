Ch 01 · Principles of Glomerulopathies

Principles of Treatment — II: Hypertension, Lipids, Thrombosis

Control of Hypertension

Value of BP control:

  • Relief of symptoms
  • Prevent hypertensive complications on other systems
  • Decrease progression of renal disease (renoprotective)

Target BP — according to degree of proteinuria:

  • If proteinuria > 1 gm/day → target BP < 125/75 (mean BP < 92)
  • If proteinuria < 1 gm/day → target BP < 130/80 (mean BP < 98)

Choice of antihypertensive agent:

  • ACEIs / ARBs
  • Calcium-channel blockers (CCB)
  • All have renoprotective effect

Control of Hyperlipidemia

  • Avoid high-fat diet
  • Physical activity
  • Use statins

Control of Hypercoagulability & Thrombosis

Prophylactic anticoagulation should be employed in nephrotic syndrome patients at high risk of thromboembolism:

• Increased risk of venous thrombosis (albumin < 2.5) → anticoagulants
• Increased risk of arterial thrombosis → aspirin
