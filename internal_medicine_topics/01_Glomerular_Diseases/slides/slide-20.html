Ch 02 · Acute GN (Nephritic Syndrome)

Treatment — 1. General Measures

General treatment

To control symptoms and prevent complications.
Home treatmentHospital admission
For uncomplicated casesFor complicated cases: severe hypertension, renal failure & oliguria, pulmonary edema, encephalopathy
Daily follow-up of blood pressureUntil hematuria, HTN & edema disappear, and proteinuria decreases
Urea and creatinine every few daysStrict bed rest in complicated cases (e.g. severe hypertension, pulmonary edema)
Salt restriction
Fluid restriction in oliguric patients: 1/2 L + volume of previous day urine
Protein restriction in renal failure: 0.6–0.8 gm/kg/day (≈ 40 gm/day for an adult patient)

Monitoring: Fluid chart (fluid intake and output) and measurement of body weight and blood pressure are part of daily monitoring.
