Ch 02 · Acute GN (Nephritic Syndrome)

Treatment — Hypertension, Emergencies & Specific Therapy

e.g. Hypertension & Edema

• Salt restriction
• Diuretics: loop diuretics
• Antihypertensive drugs — β-blockers should be used with caution, as they may precipitate pulmonary edema in patients with impending heart failure.

A. Hypertensive Encephalopathy

  • Maintain airway
  • Control blood pressure by parenteral agent, such as hydralazine 5–20 mg infusion
  • Control of fits: IV diazepam (10 mg)

B. Pulmonary Edema / Heart Failure

  • Salt and water restriction
  • Ultrafiltration (dialysis) to remove excess fluid in oliguric patient
  • Acute renal failure: dialysis
