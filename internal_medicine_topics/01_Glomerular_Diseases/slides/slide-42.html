Ch 06 · Lupus Nephritis

Management & Prognosis

Goal of management

Principal goal: normalize renal function & prevent loss of renal function.
MeasureRecommendation
HydroxychloroquineOr an equivalent antimalarial — to all patients with LN unless contraindicated.
Control of hypertensionTarget blood pressure ≤ 120/80 mmHg.
Control of proteinuriaLow doses of ACEIs or ARBs & protein restriction.
Control of hyperlipidemiaStatins and low-fat diet.
Avoid nephrotoxic drugse.g. NSAIDs. Non-acetylated salicylates are safe.
Avoid pregnancy in patients with active LNIt may worsen their renal disease; moreover, certain medications may be teratogenic.

I. General + II. Specific

Specific treatment: corticosteroids and immunosuppressive treatment of LN is divided into induction and maintenance phases.

• Corticosteroids should be instituted if the patient has clinically significant renal disease.
• Immunosuppressive agents: cyclophosphamide, mycophenolate mofetil, azathioprine, or cyclosporine A.

Prognosis

• Class I, II and V → generally good prognosis.
• Class III and IV → poor prognosis — tend to progress to ESRD, particularly class IV.
• Class VI → hemodialysis or transplantation.
