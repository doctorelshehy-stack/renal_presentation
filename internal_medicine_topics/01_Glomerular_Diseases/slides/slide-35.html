Ch 05 · Post-Streptococcal GN

Clinical Presentation

PresentationNotes
1. Acute GN: in children, picture of acute GN. In adults, the history of streptococcal infection is less commonly obtained, and the onset is subacute or insidious with progressive, slowly developing edema of the lower limbs.• About 10% of infected individuals develop GN
• Infection may be mild and pass unnoticed
• No relationship between severity of infection and probability of developing acute nephritis
2. Nephrotic syndrome: uncommon (< 20% of cases)
3. RPGN: in 5% of cases
4. Asymptomatic: discovered during routine urine examination

Key points: ~10% of infected individuals develop GN; the infection may be mild and pass unnoticed, and there is no relationship between severity of infection and the probability of developing acute nephritis.
