Ch 05 · Post-Streptococcal GN

Organism & History of Infection

Organism

Group A β-hemolytic streptococci (nephritogenic strains — types 1, 4, 12, 49).
Type 49 is the most commonly isolated type.

History of streptococcal infection

History of streptococcal infection (tonsillitis, pharyngitis, otitis media, or cellulitis) 1–2 weeks before the onset of acute nephritis.

In skin infection, the latent period may be prolonged to 4 weeks.

The latent period (time between exposure to infection and development of acute nephritis) reflects the time taken for immune-complex formation & deposition and glomerular injury.

N.B.: A shorter latent period signifies exacerbation of underlying CKD (e.g. IgA nephropathy) rather than de novo acute GN.
