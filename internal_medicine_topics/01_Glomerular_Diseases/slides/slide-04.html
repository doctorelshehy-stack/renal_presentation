Ch 01 · Principles of Glomerulopathies

Definition & Clinical Features

Definition

Term used to describe a group of glomerular disorders characterized by:

Clinically

Presence of the following features, either singly or in combination.

Pathologically

Involvement of both kidneys, either symmetrically.

Etiologically

Unknown (primary GN) or secondary to various causes (secondary GN).

Five cardinal features

  • 1. Proteinuria
  • 2. Hematuria
  • 3. Oliguria
  • 4. Hypertension
  • 5. Edema

Involvement & classification

Both kidneys are involved, either symmetrically.

• Unknown cause → Primary GN (idiopathic).
• Secondary to various causes → Secondary GN.

Key: The term glomerulopathy covers a group of glomerular disorders recognized clinically (features), pathologically (symmetric bilateral kidney involvement) and etiologically (primary vs secondary).
