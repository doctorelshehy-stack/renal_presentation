Ch 04 · Anti-GBM / Goodpasture

Definition, Pathology & Differential Diagnosis

Goodpasture Disease (Anti-GBM GN)Goodpasture's Syndrome
A kidney-limited disease with:
a) GN resulting from glomerular deposition of anti-GBM antibodies
b) Circulating anti-GBM antibodies		A systemic disease with a triad of:
a) GN resulting from glomerular deposition of anti-GBM antibodies
b) Circulating anti-GBM antibodies
c) Pulmonary hemorrhage

Pathology / Histopathology

Diffuse proliferative GN by LM, and linear deposits of anti-GBM IgG antibody by IF.

• G.P. Disease → GN
• G.P. Syndrome → GN + pulmonary hemorrhage

Differential Diagnosis

D.D. of hemoptysis with renal disease (Pulmonary-Renal Syndromes):
1. Goodpasture syndrome
2. Henoch-Schönlein purpura
3. Systemic lupus erythematosus (SLE)
4. Microscopic polyangiitis
5. Cryoglobulinemia
6. Advanced uremia with pulmonary edema and coagulopathies
7. Thrombotic thrombocytopenic purpura (TTP)
8. Pulmonary embolism with RV thrombosis
