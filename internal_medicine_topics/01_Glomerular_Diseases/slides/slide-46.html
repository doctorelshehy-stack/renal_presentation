Ch 07 · Diabetic Nephropathy

Pathology & Pathogenesis

Pathology — 3 major lesions

  • Glomerulosclerosis: diffuse (more common but non-specific) and/or nodular (less common but pathognomonic)
  • Chronic tubulo-interstitial nephritis
  • Vasculitic lesions: arteriolosclerosis in afferent & efferent arterioles (pathognomonic for DM)

Pathogenesis (multifactorial)

FactorMechanism
1. HaemodynamicHyperglycemia → ↑ GFR → ↑ intraglomerular pressure → endothelial injury → glomerulosclerosis.
2. MetabolicHyperglycemia → glycosylation of mesangial & GBM proteins → trapping of circulating macromolecules in the glomerular capillary wall and mesangium → mesangial hyperplasia → alteration of GBM permeability.
3. Genetic susceptibilityAs not all diabetic patients develop DN.
