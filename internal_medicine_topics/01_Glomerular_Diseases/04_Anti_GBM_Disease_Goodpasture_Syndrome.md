# Anti-GBM Disease & Syndrome — Goodpasture Disease & Syndrome (GPD & GPS)

**Source:** Internal Medicine Nephrology (Principles of Nephrology) – Dr. Hassan Abd-Elhady, Menoufia University

---

#### Definition

| Goodpasture Disease (Anti-GBM GN): | Good Pasture's syndrome: |
|---|---|
| • A kidney limited disease with; a) G.N. resulted from glomerular deposition of anti-GBM antibodies. b) Circulating anti-GBM antibodies. | • A systemic disease with triad of: a) G.N. resulted from glomerular deposition of anti-GBM antibodies. b) Circulating anti-GBM antibodies. c) Pulmonary hemorrhage |

---

#### Pathology/Histopathology:

Diffuse Proliferative GN by L/M, and linear deposits of anti-GBM IgG antibody by IF.

- G.P. Disease → G.N.
- G.P. Syndrome → GN + Pulmonary hemorrhage.

---

#### D.D of Hemoptysis with renal disease: (Pulmonary-Renal Syndromes):

1. Goodpasture Syndrome.
2. Henoch-Schonlen Purpura.
3. Systemic Lupus Erythematosus (SLE).
4. Microscopic Polyangitis.
5. Cryoglobinemia.
6. Advanced uremia with pulmonary edema and coagulopathies.
7. Thrombotic Thrombocytopenic Purpura (TTP).
8. Pulmonary embolism with R.V. Thrombosis.

---

#### Investigations:

1. **Urine Examination;** Evidence of G.N. i.e. hematuria with dysmorphic red cells, red cell casts and variable degrees of proteinuria.

2. **Blood:**
   - Positive circulating anti-GBM Abs.
   - Other serological tests e.g. complements, ASOT, ANA are normal.

3. **Renal biopsy:** Diffuse proliferative GN.

---

#### Treatment:

| Corticosteroids: | Cyclophosphamide: |
|---|---|
| IV methylprednisolone 7-15 mg/kg/d (maximum 1gm/d) for 3 days, then oral prednisone 1 mg/kg/d. reduce to 20 mg/d by 6 weeks & continue for 6 month. | Dose depends upon the patient's age. • <55 y, 3 mg/kg (down to 50 mg) for 3 months. • >55 y, 2 mg/kg (down to 50 mg) for 3 months. |

**Plasma exchange (plasmapheresis):**
Replacement with daily 40-50 ml/kg plasma for albumin for 2 weeks or until antiGBM antibody disappear.

**N.B:**
Plasmapharesis should not be given in patients with anuria and/or crescents >85% of glomeruli, unless there is pulmonary hemorrhage. Albumin the recommended fluid with plasmapharesis because of the lower incidence of complications

**Summary:**
3 days pulse methylprednisolone → 2 weeks course of daily Plasmapharesis → 6 months oral corticosteroids and 3 months cyclophosphamide
