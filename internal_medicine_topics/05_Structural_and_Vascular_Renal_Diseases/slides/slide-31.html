Recap

Summary — Key Points

Simple Renal Cysts

  • Common > 50 y; asymptomatic; excellent prognosis.
  • US: echoes-free, thin smooth wall, enhanced back wall; CT: no enhancement.
  • Hematuria → suspect RCC.

ADPKD

  • AD inheritance; chr 16 (ADPKD-1) & chr 4 (ADPKD-2).
  • HTN in 50%, hepatic cysts in 50%, cerebral aneurysms → SAH.
  • Treatment: control BP; cyst-penetrating antibiotics; avoid peritoneal dialysis.

ARPKD

  • AR inheritance; chr 6; parents unaffected.
  • Perinatal → pulmonary hypoplasia; early life → hepatic fibrosis / portal HTN.
  • US is test of choice (enlarged echogenic kidneys).

Hypertensive Nephrosclerosis

  • CKD complicating AHT; clinical diagnosis (5 criteria).
  • BP goal < 130/80; low salt diet; diuretics cornerstone.
  • ACEI/ARB + CCB combination; MRA fourth-line (hyperkalemia/AKI risk).

Management Principles

  • Intensive BP control; dose diuretics to GFR.
  • RAAS blockade central; avoid DHP-CCB monotherapy in proteinuric CKD.
  • Watch for hypokalemia, hyperkalemia, AKI.

Exam Pearls

  • Normal urine in ADPKD cyst infection — use blood cultures.
  • Recurrent hematuria → RCC; SAH → berry aneurysm.
  • CRF in ADPKD: avoid peritoneal dialysis.
