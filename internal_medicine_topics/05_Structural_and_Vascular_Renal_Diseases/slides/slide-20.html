Cystic Kidney Diseases · ARPKD

Symptoms — Presentation by Age

Perinatal Period

  • High mortality due to pulmonary hypoplasia.

Early Life — Liver Dominant

  • Presentation dominated by features of liver disease.
  • Hepatic cysts are uncommon, but symptoms result from hepatic fibrosis and portal hypertension (i.e. GI bleeding, hepato-splenomegaly and hypersplenism).

Increasing Age — Renal Prominent

  • Symptoms of renal involvement become prominent; e.g. abdominal mass, hypertension, recurrent UTIs, and renal failure in adolescence.

NB: ARPKD may be associated with berry aneurysms, aortic aneurysms and Ehlers-Danlos syndrome.
