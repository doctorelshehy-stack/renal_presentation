Cystic Kidney Diseases · I- Simple (Solitary) Renal Cysts

Treatment

ConditionManagement
Asymptomatic cystsPeriodic reevaluation.
Large cystMay require aspiration and alcohol instillation. If contains > 500 ml of fluid should be drained surgically.
Infected cystShould be differentiated from abscess & treated with antibiotics.
HypertensionMay improve after decompression of the cyst.

Remember: Rare hematuria should always raise a suspicion of renal cell carcinoma.
