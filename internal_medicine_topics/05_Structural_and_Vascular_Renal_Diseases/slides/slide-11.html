Cystic Kidney Diseases · ADPKD

Clinical Features — Symptoms & Causes (1/2)

SymptomCause
HematuriaDue to; rupture of a cyst into the renal pelvis, renal stone or UTI.
Renal infectionFlank pain, fever, and leukocytosis. Blood cultures may be positive and urine analysis may be normal because the cyst does not communicate directly with the urinary tract.

Exam Pearl: In ADPKD, urine analysis may be normal during cyst infection — the infected cyst does not communicate with the urinary tract; blood cultures are more reliable.
