Hypertensive Nephrosclerosis

Antihypertensive Drugs (1/2)

1- ACEIs

  • Angiotensin Converting Enzyme Inhibitors

2- ARBs

  • Angiotensin Receptor Blockers

3- Calcium Channel Blockers (CCBs)

  • Non-dihydropyridine CCBs consistently reduce albuminuria and slow the decline in kidney function.
  • Dihydropyridine CCBs should not be used as monotherapy in proteinuric CKD patients — always in combination with a RAAS blocker.

Caution: Dihydropyridine CCBs in proteinuric CKD → never as monotherapy; always combine with a RAAS blocker (ACEI or ARB).
